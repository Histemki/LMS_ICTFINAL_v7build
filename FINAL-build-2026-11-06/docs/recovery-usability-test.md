# Password recovery usability test

## Participant task

> You forgot your password for the LMS. Restore access to your account and tell us what you would do next if the recovery email does not arrive.

Do not help the participant unless they ask to stop. Observe whether they can complete the flow and identify the next step after each screen.

## Questions after the scenario

1. What should you do if the recovery email does not arrive?
2. How long is the recovery link valid?
3. What password requirements did the page show?
4. What will you need to do on your other devices after changing the password?

## Correct answers

1. Check Spam, wait five minutes, and verify the email spelling. If it still does not arrive, contact LMS support.
2. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
3. At least eight characters, including a letter and a number.
4. Sign in again on the other devices.

## Success measure

Count a participant as successful when they correctly state or perform the next step after the recovery screens. The prototype meets the target when at least 80% of participants are successful.
